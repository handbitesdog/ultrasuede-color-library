against the cloth — the archive's own photograph on the left, the shader on the right, both 418px across a 60mm swatch. tooth is the spread after everything coarser than 2.5mm is filtered out, as a percentage of the mean; the photographs have a median of 7.74% and a median skew of -0.12

one tile, 384px

grain — the size of the largest clump, in mm of cloth. Each tile is a 60mm swatch at the size a tile is really drawn; the sweep runs past the floor on purpose, so the point where fineness starts costing tooth is visible

grain, same sweep at 384px — the same cloth under a glass

grain, contrast-matched — FILL solved at each grain to hold the drawn tooth identical. The swatches should look the same amount of textured and differ only in how fine it is; the cost is in the numbers, not the thumbnails

relief — BUMP, the slope of the top of the pile, swept with the depth off so what is read is the slope's own doing. the knee is a tip angle and not a gain: about 20 degrees rms, wherever the band beneath it happens to be. 0.00 is the flat plane the shader started as, and it has no highlight anywhere: everything at the top of the range with dark specks cut out of it

tuft — TUFT_MM, the coarse end of the pile height, in mm. The lean stays at the shipping grain throughout; only the height band moves

depth — DEPTH, optical depth per standard deviation of pile height. 0.00 is an emboss with no shadow in it

clump — CLUMP, how far the lean turns uphill per unit of slope. 0.00 is the two fields uncorrelated, which is where this file was; negative leans the fibre downhill instead, away from the crest rather than into it

the same six at 4x, centre 15mm of the tile — the whole of what this knob does is at half a millimetre, which a 60mm tile on a screen cannot show

scatter — SCATTER, how far the lean's lattice is jittered by the pile's slope, in cells. 0.00 is the fault: gradient noise is exactly zero at every lattice point, so one octave of it draws a crosshatch

the same six at 2x, so the lattice is visible at all

the two maps a 3D pipeline wants — surface normal (the pile's relief) and fibre direction (which way it leans). Different maps, both tiling

ambient fill — how loud the nap is; 1.00 is flat dye

light elevation — what shape the nap takes, at the shipping fill
